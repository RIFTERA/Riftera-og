The World

One night, the sky
came apart.

Nobody in Riftera remembers a sky without the scar. A generation ago the heavens split along a seam of violet fire, and through it poured the raw weather of another world — storms that think, light that cuts, islands that forgot how to fall.

The kingdoms did not survive the night intact. They survived changed. Castles ride the wind on shards of their own mountains. Rivers run uphill toward the rift, glowing cyan with borrowed lightning. And in the ruins, three orders rose from the ash — each certain it alone knows what the rift is for.

Now the seam is widening again. New rifts open over the Shattered Isles every season, and whatever steps through them next will decide whether Riftera is healed, conquered — or unmade. The next chapter is not written by kings. It is minted.

LOC.01

The Shattered Isles

A thousand castle-topped shards adrift above the clouds, chained together with bridges of woven lightning. Home of the Riftwardens — and the first place the sky broke.

LOC.02

The Riftstorm

A permanent storm wrapped around the great seam, where the weather has a memory and the lightning strikes in patterns. Stormcallers sail it. No one else survives it.

LOC.03

The Undervale

The shadowed lowlands beneath the floating world, where things that fell through the rift learned to walk. The Voidborne rule here, and they are done hiding.

Choose your side

Three orders. One broken world.

Every Rifter belongs to one of the three orders fighting over the rift — Riftwardens of the shield, Stormcallers of the bolt, Voidborne of the moon. Your faction is written into your NFT's traits, alongside its realm, rune, aura and power.

The Riftwardens

Guardians of the Seam · Shield

Pixel master · Rose Knight

Knights sworn to stand where the sky broke. Their armor is quenched in rift-light until the violet runs through the steel itself. They do not ask what is coming through. They ask where to stand.

The Stormcallers

Sailors of the Riftstorm · Bolt

Pixel master · Tempest Bard

Mages who read the storm like scripture and ride it like a sea. To a Stormcaller the rift is not a wound — it is a door, and doors are for opening. Their lightning answers before thunder does.

The Voidborne

Children of the Other Side · Moon

Pixel master · Eclipse Oracle

They came through the seam — or were made by it. Half in this world, half still dissolving into the next, the Voidborne fight with blades of solid rift-light and a patience no mortal order can match.

Questions from the Vale

Before you cross

What do I need to mint?

A wallet on BNB Chain holding a Binance Agent ID (.ai) NFT. The Agent ID gates both lanes: up to 3 free mints and up to 5 paid mints per wallet. If your agent holds your Agentic Wallet, it can mint for you with the prompt above.

How much does it cost?

The Free Gate costs 0 BNB (gas only): 444 Rifters, IDs 1–444, max 3 per wallet. The Rift Gate costs exactly 0.001 BNB per mint: 667 Rifters, IDs 1001–1667, max 5 per wallet. Both lanes open in parallel on day one.

What is the difference between free and paid Rifters?

Everything you can see. Free Rifters are Common only — muted chibi pixel art in a thin grey frame, Power 100–399. Paid Rifters are bright chibi pixel editions in a chunky double frame, with Power up to 999 — and only the paid lane can pull Rare (cyan frame) or Legendary (gold frame, crown-corner diamonds). Paid Rifters also unlock the full Rift Shards game.

Can a free mint be Rare or Legendary?

No. All 167 Rares and all 33 Legendaries live exclusively in the paid lane. A free mint is a Common with certainty — the paid lane is where the 15% and the 3% live.

What does a Rifter actually do?

Your Rifter is your identity in the world — faction, realm, rune, aura and power, minted on-chain — and your key to the game. Paid-edition holders get full access to Rift Shards at launch through a wallet check; free-edition holders and visitors play the demo.

When does the mint open?

Soon — the contract is written and tested, and deployment is the final step. There is no official contract address yet; the address, and only the address, is announced here at launch. Any address circulating before that announcement is fake.

Which faction and traits will my mint get?

One of the three orders — Riftwardens, Stormcallers or Voidborne — combined with a realm, a rune, an aura and a power score. No two of the 1,111 Rifters share the same combination. Your agent reports your token's traits after the mint.
